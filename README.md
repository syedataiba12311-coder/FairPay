# FairPay – Your AI-Powered Financial Decision Assistant 💳

### Don't just ask "Can I afford it?" Ask "Should I buy it now?"

## 💡 The Vision

FairPay was built around a simple but important idea: **making smarter financial decisions should be easier for everyone.**

The vision was to develop an AI-powered personal financial assistant that goes beyond checking someone's bank balance. Instead of simply telling users whether they have enough money to purchase something, FairPay aims to understand their overall financial situation and determine whether making that purchase is actually a wise decision.

Whether someone wants to buy a laptop, purchase a phone, or make a major expense, FairPay aims to answer one important question: **Can I afford this without putting my financial stability at risk?**

## 🚀 What FairPay Aims to Achieve

The proposed system is designed to consider multiple financial factors, including:

- **Current Balance:** Understand the money currently available.
- **Recurring Expenses:** Account for rent, bills, subscriptions and other regular commitments.
- **Pending Payments:** Consider upcoming financial obligations.
- **Expected Income:** Include confirmed future income in financial planning.
- **Essential Spending:** Protect money needed for daily necessities.
- **Payment Options:** Evaluate full payments, partial payments and available instalment options.
- **Financial Context:** Eventually interpret relevant information from messages, receipts and images to build a more complete financial picture.

Based on these factors, FairPay aims to recommend whether a user should buy now, purchase through instalments, wait, or avoid the purchase altogether.

## 🎯 The Innovation

What makes FairPay different is its focus on **financial affordability rather than just available balance**.

A person might have enough money in their account to purchase an expensive laptop but still have rent, bills and other essential payments due next week.

FairPay's vision is to identify these hidden financial risks before a purchase is made and help users make decisions with greater confidence.

## 🛠️ Development Journey & Limitations

This project represents my attempt to turn that vision into a working prototype.

Due to limited development facilities, resources and time, I could not complete every feature or achieve the level of integration and reliability I originally envisioned. Some advanced capabilities, particularly the complete financial data integration and comprehensive real-world validation, remain part of the future development roadmap.

However, the project demonstrates the concept, its intended functionality and the potential of combining AI with practical financial decision-making.

I see these limitations not as the end of the idea, but as opportunities for further development.

## 🔮 Future Vision

My long-term goal is to evolve FairPay into a comprehensive personal financial companion that can:

- Connect securely with banking and payment platforms.
- Analyse financial transactions in real time.
- Understand receipts, screenshots and financial messages.
- Predict future cash-flow shortages.
- Compare payment and instalment options.
- Provide personalised financial recommendations.
- Help users develop healthier spending habits.

## ❤️ A Final Note

FairPay started with a simple question: *Just because you can afford something today, does that mean you can afford it tomorrow?*

I believe technology should not only make payments easier but also help people make better financial decisions.

**FairPay is my attempt to bring that vision to life.**

*Built with ambition, developed with limited resources, and designed with a bigger vision in mind.*
